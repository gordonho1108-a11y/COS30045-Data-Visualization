Televisions: a household energy habit

Televisions are switched on for hours at a time in most Australian homes, which makes their running cost easy to underestimate. Screen size, display technology and picture settings all change how many watts a television actually draws. Use the calculator below to estimate the cost of running a television — or any other appliance — over a day, month or year.
